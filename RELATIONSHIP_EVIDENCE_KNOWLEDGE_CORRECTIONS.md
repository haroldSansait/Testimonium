# Caloy knowledge correction proposal: relationship-based evidence

Status: local proposal only. Do not import, approve, publish, or embed this material in production without the existing knowledge-governance review.

## Authoritative replacement

For a TESTIMONIUM sacramental document request, Self keeps the existing document checklist and account-level valid_id verification. Every other canonical relationship — Parent, Child, Spouse, Sibling, Other Relative, or Authorized Representative — requires three additive items before submission:

1. a signed authorization letter;
2. a clear JPEG or PNG image of the front of the registrant's Government ID; and
3. a clear JPEG or PNG image of the back of the registrant's Government ID.

Each ID image is limited to 5 MB. The images support relationship evidence for that request and do not replace or change the requester's account-level Government ID verification. Automated OCR is advisory only; parish staff remains responsible for review. If the registrant cannot provide both ID sides or authorization, the requester should contact the selected parish office before submitting.

## Existing corpus statements to retire

- In corpus/cp6_initial_corpus.json, retire the statement that parents or legal guardians do not require an authorization letter and need only their own identification.
- In corpus/cp6_comprehensive_system_corpus.json, retire the same minor-child exemption and the claim that this evidence is restricted to adult Authorized Representative requests.
- Replace generic “valid ID image” guidance with a distinction between account-level valid_id verification and request-specific registrant-ID front/back evidence.
- Do not tell users that the designated representative's ID is uploaded as part of this feature; the implemented request-specific images are the registrant's ID.

## Suggested answers

Question: I am requesting my child's baptismal certificate. What do I upload?

Answer: Select Parent. In addition to the parish's ordinary certificate requirements, upload a signed authorization letter and clear front and back JPEG/PNG images of the registrant's Government ID, up to 5 MB per image. If the registrant has no suitable ID or cannot provide authorization, contact the selected parish office before submitting.

Question: Does this replace my verified ID?

Answer: No. Your account-level Government ID verification remains separate. The additional front/back images are relationship evidence for the person whose record is being requested.

Question: Does OCR approve the request?

Answer: No. OCR only helps staff review the uploaded evidence. It does not approve identity, payment, or the document request.
